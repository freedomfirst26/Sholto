## Questions

**Can I use it at a paid gig?** Yes, free, as many as you like.

**Can my club or bar run it for the DJs who play there?** Yes, free.

**Can I stream or teach with it?** Yes, free.

**Can I give copies to friends?** Yes, with the licence text and Required Notice
lines. Don't charge for it or market it as a substitute for Sholto.

**Can I fork it?** Yes. You can fork it, change it, and use your fork yourself. You
need a commercial licence only if you provide your fork (or anything built from it)
as a product that competes with Sholto, even for free. Using it is never competing;
providing a competing product is.

**Can I sell it, rebrand it, host it, or ship it with a controller or other product?**
Not without a commercial licence: <freedomfirst26@proton.me>.

**Does Sholto ever change licence on a date?** No. There is no date on which Sholto changes
licence. The earlier BSL releases (v0.3.0 and v1.0.0) convert to Apache-2.0 on their own
Change Dates, as their licence says.
